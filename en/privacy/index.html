Privacy policy

Updated: 25 October 2026

Developer: Muhammet Ali Kaymaz

TemizMesaj classifies eligible incoming messages on your device. Message content, sender details and local reports are not sent to the developer.

The app does not scan message history, delete messages or filter iMessage. iOS decides which new messages are eligible for third-party filtering.

The reporting extension creates local sender rules. It does not send a central spam report to the developer.

Rules and settings are stored locally. Optional iCloud sync encrypts them; the key is stored in iCloud Keychain. Purchase access is not included in this sync.

Exported rule files contain the sender or content matches you entered. You choose where to save or share them. Deleting app data does not delete exported copies.

A custom HTTPS feed sends a request to the address you select. Its server can see your IP address, but the request contains no message text.

To learn new scam terms, the app downloads the public rule list from this site at most twice a day. The request contains no message text, sender or personal data; the host (GitHub) may see standard connection logs such as the IP address.

Purchases and subscriptions use Apple StoreKit. The app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

The delete action clears local rules and the iCloud settings record. Repeat on other devices so old settings are not synced back.

Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel it in your Apple account subscriptions.

If you email support, your email address and the information you provide are used to answer your request. Do not send verification codes, payment details or private messages. Contact the support address for privacy requests.

This website uses no analytics, advertising, external fonts or tracking cookies. If published, the hosting provider may process standard connection logs to serve the pages.
